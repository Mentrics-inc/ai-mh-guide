<!-- explainer from 2026-coursera-lund-ai-applications-mental-health-training.md · 3s --> # Understanding AI in Mental Health This course, offered by Lund University via Coursera, explores how artificial intelligence is transforming mental health care. It bridges the gap between traditional clinical practice and modern technology, focusing on practical applications rather than just theoretical computer science. The content is designed for clinicians and students who want to understand how AI tools can support, rather than replace, human judgment in therapy and diagnosis. ## Why It Matters Mental health professionals face increasing demand and complex cases. AI offers new ways to assess patients more accurately and personalize treatment plans. However, these tools also raise important questions about privacy, bias, and the role of human empathy in care. Understanding these dynamics is crucial for anyone involved in modern healthcare delivery. This course helps users navigate these changes by explaining both the benefits and the limitations of current AI technologies.
