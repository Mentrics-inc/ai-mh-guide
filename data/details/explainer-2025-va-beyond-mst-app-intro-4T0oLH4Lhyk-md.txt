It is a serious issue that affects many veterans, but support is available. The Department of Veterans Affairs promotes the "Beyond MST" app, a free, secure, and private digital tool designed to help veterans manage their recovery and well-being. This explainer outlines what the resource offers and why it is significant for those who have experienced MST. **Why It Matters** Experiencing MST can lead to long-term emotional and psychological challenges. Accessible, confidential support is crucial for healing. The Beyond MST app addresses this need by providing immediate, on-demand resources. It empowers users to take control of their recovery journey from anywhere, removing barriers to care such as stigma or logistical difficulties.
