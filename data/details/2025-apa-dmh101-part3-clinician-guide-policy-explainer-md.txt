<!-- explainer from 2025-apa-dmh101-part3-clinician-guide-policy.pdf --> # Understanding Digital Mental Health Tools Digital mental health tools, such as mobile apps, are becoming common aids in therapy. However, they are not a replacement for professional care and come with unique ethical and legal considerations that both clinicians and patients must understand. # Why It Matters Unlike traditional medical records, many health apps do not offer the same level of privacy protection or legal oversight. Research shows that even popular apps often have significant privacy flaws. Using these tools without careful evaluation can lead to data breaches, wasted time, or delays in receiving more effective treatments. Furthermore, patients may mistakenly assume apps are bound by the same strict privacy laws as healthcare providers, which is often not the case. # Key Takeaways 1. **Review Privacy Policies:** Always read the app’s privacy policy to understand how your personal data is stored, used, and shared.
