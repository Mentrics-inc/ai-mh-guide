<!-- explainer from 2026-unc-core-components-trauma-informed-practice-training.md · 5s --> # Understanding Trauma-Informed Practice This resource outlines a free, self-paced online course developed by the UNC School of Social Work and the National Child Traumatic Stress Network. Designed for mental health professionals, the curriculum teaches a standardized approach to helping children, youth, and families who have experienced trauma. The course is structured around twelve core practice elements that are commonly found in evidence-based trauma treatments. These elements guide practitioners through the entire care journey, starting with assessment and psychoeducation, moving through safety planning and emotional regulation, and concluding with trauma processing and termination rituals. # Why This Matters Trauma affects how people think, feel, and behave. Without a structured approach, care can feel disjointed or even re-traumatizing. This curriculum provides a consistent "scaffold" for providers, ensuring that every step of treatment is intentional and supportive.
