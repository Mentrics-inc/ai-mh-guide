# CDC — Essentials for Childhood: Preventing ACEs through Data to Action Source: https://www.cdc.gov/aces/programs/index.html Captured: 2026-09-12 Category: abuse-treatment Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- CDC funds recipients to prevent adverse childhood experiences (ACEs) and promote positive childhood experiences (PCEs).
