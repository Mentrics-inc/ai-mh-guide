The PE Coach app is a digital companion designed to support Prolonged Exposure (PE) therapy, an evidence-based treatment for Posttraumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD). Rather than replacing professional care, this tool helps patients stay organized and engaged between visits with their mental health provider. It serves as a centralized hub for managing appointments, tracking progress, and practicing coping skills. # Why It Matters Consistency is crucial for the effectiveness of PTSD treatment. The app reduces the administrative burden of therapy by helping users manage their schedule and homework in one place. By simplifying logistics, it allows patients to focus more energy on their healing process. Additionally, the app promotes privacy and reduces distractions during critical exercises, ensuring that patients can engage fully with their therapy tasks.
