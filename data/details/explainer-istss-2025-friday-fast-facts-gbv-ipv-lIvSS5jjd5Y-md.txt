<!-- explainer from istss-2025-friday-fast-facts-gbv-ipv-transcript-yt-lIvSS5jjd5Y.txt · 4s --> # Understanding Gender-Based Violence Gender-based violence (GBV) is a broad term describing harmful acts committed against a person’s will, driven by unequal power dynamics between genders. It encompasses physical, verbal, sexual, psychological, socioeconomic, financial, cultural, and vicarious abuse. A specific subset of GBV is intimate partner violence (IPV), which involves abuse—such as physical harm, coercion, financial control, or stalking—by current, former, or prospective romantic partners. Importantly, IPV can affect anyone, regardless of their demographic background. The consequences of GBV are severe and far-reaching. Beyond immediate physical and mental health impacts, survivors often face financial loss, social isolation, and disruptions to their employment and education. Legal issues, including criminal justice involvement and potential loss of child custody, are common.
