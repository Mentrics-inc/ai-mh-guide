This study evaluates whether the Brief Resilience Scale is an accurate and consistent tool for measuring the ability to recover from stress in older Iranian adults. Researchers translated the survey into Farsi and tested it with 330 older adults in Tehran using statistical methods to check for validity and reliability. The results show that the Farsi version is a dependable and valid instrument for assessing resilience in this population.
