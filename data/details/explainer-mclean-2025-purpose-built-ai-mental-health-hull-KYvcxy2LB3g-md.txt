Instead, it advocates for building foundation models specifically trained on sensitive, anonymized therapeutic data. This approach addresses the long-standing shortage of mental health providers and fills the gap for individuals needing support below the level of a clinical diagnosis. By grounding AI in psychology rather than general internet data, developers can create tools that prioritize user well-being over simple instruction following. **Why It Matters** Generic AI models are designed to please users and provide information, which can be dangerous in mental health contexts.
