# StrongMinds — Group IPT for Depression at Scale (Africa) Category: minority-populations Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- StrongMinds is a global non-profit that democratizes access to mental health care for people with depression, focusing on Africa. Key model: layperson-led Interpersonal Group Therapy (IPT-G). ## Key Statistics (as of 2026) - 2+ million people treated for depression since 2013 - $10.55 current cost to treat one person - 74% of participants are depression-free after therapy ends - 28% increase in people not missing work at 12 months - 27% increase in adolescents reporting "good" grades after therapy - 82% reported having someone to turn to for support (up from 63%) - +0.5 additional meals per day for clients' children (1.8 → 2.3) ## Approach 1. **Innovation**: Free, community-based mental health care models in low-resource African settings 2. **Partnerships**: Governments and NGOs to integrate IPT-G into existing programs 3. **Advocacy**: Global development community policy engagement 4. **Research**: Obsessive result tracking to refine the model ## Countries Uganda, Zambia, Kenya (Mombasa County), Tanzania, Malawi, Rwanda (launching 2026) ## Key Programs - Women's
