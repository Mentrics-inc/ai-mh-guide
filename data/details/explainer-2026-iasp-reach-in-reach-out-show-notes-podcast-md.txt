<!-- explainer from 2026-iasp-reach-in-reach-out-show-notes-podcast.txt · 6s --> # Understanding IASP’s “Reach In, Reach Out” Podcast The International Association for Suicide Prevention (IASP) produces the “Reach In, Reach Out” podcast series to foster global understanding of suicide. The content covers diverse topics, including responsible media reporting, the specific risks faced by women and girls, the role of helplines, and emerging tools like artificial intelligence. It also explores workplace safety, community-based interventions, and the impact of suicide loss on families. By featuring experts, policymakers, and individuals with lived experience, the series aims to reduce stigma and encourage open, compassionate dialogue. This topic matters because suicide is a significant global public health issue influenced by social, economic, and environmental factors. Misinformation and stigma often prevent people from seeking help. By examining evidence-based strategies and sharing personal stories, these discussions help shift the narrative from shame to support.
