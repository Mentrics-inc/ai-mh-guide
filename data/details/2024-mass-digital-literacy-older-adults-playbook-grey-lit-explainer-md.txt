<!-- explainer from 2024-mass-digital-literacy-older-adults-playbook-grey-lit.pdf --> # Bridging the Digital Divide for Seniors The Enhancing Digital Literacy for Older Adults (EDLOA) initiative is a two-year program in Massachusetts designed to close the "digital divide." Funded by the American Rescue Plan Act, it helps older adults in rural and diverse communities gain essential technology skills. By supporting local councils and partners, the program ensures seniors are not left behind as daily life moves online. # Why It Matters Digital inclusion is vital for independence. Without basic skills, older adults may struggle to access healthcare, connect with family, or manage finances. This initiative focuses on more than just teaching button-clicking; it builds confidence and social connection. It addresses three key barriers: the need for personalized, patient instruction; the requirement for accessible tools that accommodate physical changes like vision loss; and the high cost of devices and internet service.
