# AVATAR and SloMo Digital Therapies for Psychosis — NICE Recommended for NHS Category: psychiatry-general Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- NICE has recommended two digital health technologies for psychosis developed at King's College London's IoPPN, as part of its Early Value Assessment (EVA) for medtech initiative. ## SloMo (previously SlowMo) - **Purpose**: Managing distressing thoughts or paranoia in psychosis - **First UKCA-marked digital therapeutic for treating paranoia** - **Design**: Inclusive, digitally supported therapy; accompanies patients and therapists during in-person sessions, extends into daily life - **How it works**: Visualizes thoughts as fast-spinning grey bubbles (unhelpful fast thinking); slow-spinning colored bubbles help shrink fears and feel safer - **Evidence**: Largest RCT of its kind found SloMo reduced worries, improved self-concept and quality of life; effects sustained over 6 months; overcame the "digital divide" with strong outcomes for marginalised groups - **Implementation**: Being rolled out in South London and Maudsley NHS Foundation Trust, Sussex Partnership NHS Foundation Trust, and Cumbria, Northumberland Tyne and Wear NHS Foundation Trust - *
