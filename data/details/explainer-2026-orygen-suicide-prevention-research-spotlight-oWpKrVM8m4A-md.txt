In Australia, suicide is the leading cause of death for individuals aged 15 to 25, with rates rising sharply, particularly among young women and girls. The organization emphasizes that while suicide is preventable, past efforts have often failed because they were poorly targeted or lacked scientific evidence. Orygen aims to fix this by studying the specific drivers of self-harm and testing interventions designed specifically for at-risk youth. # Why This Matters Traditional health settings often fail to reach young people where they are most vulnerable. By moving research and support out of hospitals and into schools and online spaces, Orygen addresses a critical gap in care.
