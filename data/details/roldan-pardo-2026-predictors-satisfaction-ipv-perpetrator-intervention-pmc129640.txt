This study examines factors that influence how satisfied men are with court-mandated programs designed to address intimate partner violence. Researchers analyzed data from 761 male participants using statistical models to identify key predictors of their satisfaction levels. The main finding is that satisfaction is significantly predicted by immigrant status, cocaine use, sexist attitudes, community involvement, and the quality of the relationship with therapists, with higher-risk individuals often reporting greater satisfaction.
