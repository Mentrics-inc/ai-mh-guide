This study tested whether a culturally adapted AI chatbot based on cognitive behavioral therapy can reduce depression and loneliness among Chinese university students, particularly those facing financial stress. Researchers randomly assigned 100 students to either use the chatbot for seven days or wait for the intervention, measuring their mental health at the start, after three days, and after seven days. The chatbot significantly lowered depression and loneliness levels, with students experiencing high financial stress showing the greatest improvement.
