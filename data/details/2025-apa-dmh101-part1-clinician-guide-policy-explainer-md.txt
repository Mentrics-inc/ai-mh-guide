<!-- explainer from 2025-apa-dmh101-part1-clinician-guide-policy.pdf --> # What Is Digital Mental Health? Digital mental health, often called mHealth, refers to using smartphones, apps, and online tools to support mental well-being. While traditional healthcare relies on in-person visits, these digital solutions offer convenient, patient-driven access to self-management tools and support. With nearly half of mental health patients lacking treatment access yet most owning smartphones, this technology represents a major shift in how care is delivered. It aims to improve outcomes by making support more flexible and accessible, serving as either a primary resource or an auxiliary tool alongside professional care. # Why It Matters This shift matters because it bridges the gap between patient need and available resources. Digital tools can strengthen the relationship between patients and clinicians, potentially lowering costs and improving clinical results. However, the market is vast and unregulated.
