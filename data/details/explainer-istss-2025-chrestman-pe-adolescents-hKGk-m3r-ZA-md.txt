The conversation explains how PE helps treat Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) by reducing avoidance behaviors. Unlike standard adult PE, the adolescent version (PEA) includes a critical pre-treatment phase focused on case management and motivational interviewing. This ensures logistical barriers, like transportation, are resolved and helps reluctant teens feel a sense of control and buy-in.
