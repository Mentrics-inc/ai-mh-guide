This study investigates whether an art therapy program assisted by generative artificial intelligence can improve thinking skills and reduce anxiety and depression in older adults living in the community who have mild memory problems. The researchers conducted a 12-week trial with 80 older adults, comparing those who participated in the AI art sessions against a control group that continued their usual community activities. The results showed that participants in the intervention group experienced significant improvements in cognitive function and reductions in anxiety and depression, with these benefits largely lasting three months after the program ended.
