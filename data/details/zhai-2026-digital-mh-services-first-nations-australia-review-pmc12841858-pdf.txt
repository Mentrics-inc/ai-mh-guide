This study reviews how well digital mental health services work for Indigenous Australians and identifies the factors that help or hinder their use. The researchers analyzed 22 studies using a systematic search of academic databases to gather evidence on service effectiveness and implementation challenges. They found that these digital services are most effective for mild mental health issues, but success depends on cultural appropriateness, easy access, and strong organizational support.
