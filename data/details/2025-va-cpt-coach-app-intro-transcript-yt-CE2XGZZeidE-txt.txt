CPT helps you identify and change ways of thinking&nbsp; that get in the way of recovery from PTSD. CPT Coach is a helpful tool&nbsp; for people with PTSD who are&nbsp;&nbsp; receiving Cognitive Processing Therapy&nbsp; from a mental health care professional. Let’s explore the main features of the app. You can also work with your health care team&nbsp;&nbsp; to find the sections of the app&nbsp; that may be most useful to you. The Practice Plan is designed to help you get&nbsp; the most out of Cognitive Processing Therapy. To use the Practice Plan, select the&nbsp; type of CPT you will be completing. Start Level 1 of the plan while you are&nbsp; in the first session with your therapist. Level 1 includes several assessments,&nbsp; readings and tasks for you to work through. A checkmark will appear next&nbsp; to each task you’ve completed. Your therapist will let you know when you’re ready&nbsp; to move on to the next level of the Practice Plan.
