I'll be presenting on autobiographical editing in clients with complex trauma. What is exactly autobiographical editing or AE? When we are working with complex trauma clients, reprocessing a traumatic memory may not readily translate into significant changes in the way they see themselves and their lives. It is as if AIP were not restored enough to enable them to regard their past from a more positive integrated perspective. In my presentation, we'll learn about some options therapists may resort to in the role of editor, especially during phase eight, the forgotten phase, to help these clients acknowledge their heroic paths.
