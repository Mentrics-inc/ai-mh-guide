CLINICAL RESEARCH ARTICLE Psychometric evaluation of the PTSD Checklist for the DSM-5 (PCL-5) in Norway Espen Rasmussen Lassen a,b, Aurora Norebø Omre a, Marianne Skogbrott Birkeland a,b, Karina Egeland a and Harald Bækkelund a,c aSection for Implementation and Treatment Research, Norwegian Centre for Violence and Traumatic Stress Studies, Oslo, Norway; bDepartment of Psychology, University of Oslo, Oslo, Norway; cResearch Institute, Modum Bad Psychiatric Hospital, Vikersund, Norway ABSTRACT Background: The Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) Checklist for DSM–5 (PCL-5) is one of the most frequently applied self-report measures of PTSD symptoms. Yet, there is a need for more knowledge of the instruments’ psychometric performance in clinical samples. Furthermore, the Norwegian version of the PCL-5 has not yet been validated. Objective: This study aims to test the construct validity, internal consistency, factor structure, and change sensitivity of the Norwegian version of the Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) Checklist for DSM–5 (PCL-5) in a nationwide heterogeneous psychiatric outpatient sample in Norway.
