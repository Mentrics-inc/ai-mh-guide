This paper reviews the major historical shifts in treating mental health and substance use disorders, focusing on how these changes have improved patient engagement and care. The authors analyze key developments in medication, therapy, and policy through structured timelines to illustrate the evolution of treatment approaches. The study highlights that advancements such as psychotropic drugs and patient-centered policies have significantly enhanced care quality and restored autonomy for individuals with these conditions.
