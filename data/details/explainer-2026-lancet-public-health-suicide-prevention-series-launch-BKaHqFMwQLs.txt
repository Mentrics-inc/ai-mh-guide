<!-- explainer from 2026-lancet-public-health-suicide-prevention-series-launch-transcript-yt-BKaHqFMwQLs.txt · 15s --> # A Public Health Approach to Suicide Prevention This webinar introduces a new series in *The Lancet Public Health*, arguing that suicide prevention must move beyond clinical mental health care to a broader public health model. The authors emphasize that suicide is driven by social and commercial determinants—such as poverty, unemployment, and industries like alcohol or firearms—rather than just individual psychology. They propose a "whole-of-government" strategy where sectors like housing, finance, and education collaborate to address these root causes, rather than relying solely on health departments. ### Why It Matters Current global efforts have stalled. While suicide rates have slightly declined, they are not dropping fast enough to meet World Health Organization targets. The series highlights that focusing only on treating individuals in crisis is insufficient. By addressing upstream societal factors, governments can prevent suicides before they occur.
