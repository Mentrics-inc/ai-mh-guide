He explains that while PTSD symptoms do not disappear entirely, treatment provides the tools to manage them effectively. ### Why It Matters PTSD is a common condition among veterans and others who have experienced trauma. A major challenge for many is the feeling of being overwhelmed by intense emotions, such as anger or fear. Seitz’s story highlights a crucial shift in perspective: treatment is not about erasing feelings or achieving a state of permanent calm. Instead, it is about gaining control over reactions.
