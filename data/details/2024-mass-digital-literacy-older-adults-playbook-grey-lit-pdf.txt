This paper outlines the Enhancing Digital Literacy for Older Adults initiative, a two-year program in Massachusetts designed to bridge the digital divide for seniors. The project distributed $1.8 million to 24 local organizations, serving 3,045 older adults by providing in-person training and distributing 1,311 devices. The findings highlight that personalized, accessible support fosters greater digital confidence, social connection, and independence among older participants.
