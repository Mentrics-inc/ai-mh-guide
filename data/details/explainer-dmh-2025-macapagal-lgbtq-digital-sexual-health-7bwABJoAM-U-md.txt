With rising anti-LGBTQ+ legislation and high rates of mental health distress in this demographic, traditional in-person health education often fails to reach them effectively. Digital platforms offer a vital, private avenue for these teens to access accurate health information, build community, and learn about sexual health without immediate adult mediation. This presentation highlights how researchers can move beyond traditional methods to create interventions that teens actually use. By meeting young people where they are—on platforms like Discord, Instagram, and TikTok—researchers can co-design tools that are relevant, respectful, and effective.
