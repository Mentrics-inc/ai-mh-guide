<b>Background</b>: Positive and existential psychiatry are approaches to mental health that emphasize the promotion of well-being, resilience, and optimal functioning alongside the conventional management of mental illness. Research suggests that the development of self-regulatory metacompetencies is associated with positive mental health and well-being outcomes. Artificial intelligence (AI) technologies are increasingly being used as assistive tools in psychiatry. However, the integration of AI in therapeutic interventions remains underexplored. <b>Objectives</b>: Thus, this systematic review aimed to synthesize evidence from randomized controlled trials evaluating whether AI-based positive and existential psychiatry interventions contribute to improvements in mental and emotional health.
