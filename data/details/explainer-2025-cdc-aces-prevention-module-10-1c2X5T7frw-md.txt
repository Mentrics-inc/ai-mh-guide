It moves beyond blaming individual parents, focusing instead on the broader social and economic conditions that shape family life. ### Why It Matters Early experiences significantly influence a child’s long-term health, productivity, and overall life potential. However, these outcomes are not determined solely by genetics or parenting choices. Instead, they result from a balance between "risk factors" (such as poverty, discrimination, or lack of resources) and "protective factors" (such as stable relationships and safe environments). Recognizing this balance helps society address the root causes of inequality rather than stigmatizing families who struggle due to systemic barriers. ### Key Takeaways 1. **The Scale Analogy**: Think of development as a scale.
