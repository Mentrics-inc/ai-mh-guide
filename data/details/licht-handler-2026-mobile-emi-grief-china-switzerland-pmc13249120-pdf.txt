This paper outlines a study to design mobile phone apps that provide grief support tailored to the cultural contexts of China and Switzerland. The researchers are using a collaborative method that involves interviewing bereaved individuals and experts in both countries to gather feedback on the app content. The study is currently recruiting participants and analyzing their responses to identify key features for the interventions, with final results expected by the end of 2026.
