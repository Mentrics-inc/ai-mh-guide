This paper reviews methods for the early detection of autism spectrum disorder in young children, covering approaches from traditional clinical screening to multimodal artificial intelligence. The authors conducted a scoping review of 65 studies published between 2010 and 2026 to analyze evidence across biomedical, psychological, and engineering databases. The main finding is that while AI and digital tools show promise for objective quantification, they currently face significant limitations regarding data quality and validation, and should be developed as supportive tools for clinicians rather than standalone diagnostic systems.
