This study explores how women from migrant backgrounds use digital platforms for perinatal mental health support. Researchers conducted interviews and focus groups with women from Chinese, Arabic, and Indian-language speaking communities to analyze their interactions with five online resources. The main finding is that while these tools are valued for privacy and convenience, their effectiveness is limited by poor language translation, lack of cultural relevance, and late introduction into care.
