This paper examines whether online Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing therapy can help children recover from traumatic events. The researchers used a qualitative case study approach to analyze two young clients and their parents, tracking their progress with standardized symptom tests. The main finding was that the online therapy successfully reduced the children's post-traumatic symptoms, with these improvements lasting for an extended period.
