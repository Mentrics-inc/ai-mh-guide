Individuals with mood or psychotic disorders face high readmission rates, leading to poorer health outcomes and increased costs. The research focuses on using machine learning to analyze electronic health records (EHRs) to identify those at highest risk. **Why It Matters** Current prediction models often fail because they are tested only within single hospitals. This study evaluates whether these tools work across different hospital systems, which is crucial for widespread implementation. The research combines structural data (like billing codes) with unstructured text (like discharge notes) to create more robust, scalable prediction algorithms. **Key Takeaways** 1. **Timeline Matters:** Researchers are developing models that explicitly track the timeline of a patient’s medical history.
