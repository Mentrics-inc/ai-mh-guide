This pilot study examined the feasibility and preliminary outcomes of an online group therapy program for adult women with histories of childhood sexual abuse. The researchers treated nine participants using a combination of trauma-focused cognitive behavioral therapy and eye movement desensitization and reprocessing delivered in different sequences. Although the small sample size prevented statistically significant results, the findings suggest the approach is feasible and may improve symptoms like PTSD and self-esteem.
