This study investigates how digital technology is used to commit gender-based violence against women with disabilities in South Africa. Researchers analyzed survey responses from 204 women to examine the types of online abuse they faced and how factors like income, education, and disability type influenced their experiences. The results show that cyberbullying, hacking, and hate speech are the most common forms of this violence, with women facing greater risks when they also experience poverty and limited access to safe technology.
