This study examines how changes in a person's ability to accept difficult thoughts and feelings affect intimate partner violence among men in a court-mandated therapy program. Researchers tracked 56 participants over three time points to measure shifts in psychological flexibility and violent behaviors. The results showed that as men became more open to their emotions, their violent actions significantly decreased, suggesting this mental shift is a key driver of positive change.
