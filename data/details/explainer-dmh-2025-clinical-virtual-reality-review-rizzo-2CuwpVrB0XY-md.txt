Led by an expert from the University of Southern California, the presentation explains how immersive technology helps treat anxiety, PTSD, and physical injuries. It allows patients to confront fears or practice skills in a setting that feels real enough to trigger emotional responses, yet safe enough to prevent harm. This approach makes therapy more accessible and effective, particularly for conditions like PTSD where traditional imagination-based therapy can be difficult for patients to engage with.
