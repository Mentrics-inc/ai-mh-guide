<!-- explainer from 2026-understanding-psychosis-show-notes-podcast.txt · 6s --> # Understanding Psychosis: A Plain-Language Guide **What It Covers** The "Understanding Psychosis" podcast, produced by the Schizophrenia & Psychosis Action Alliance, serves as an educational hub for individuals, families, and clinicians navigating psychosis. Rather than focusing solely on medical definitions, the show centers on lived experience. It features conversations with people who have personally experienced psychosis, their family members, and healthcare providers. The content explores the emotional reality of early psychosis, offering practical advice on communication, crisis management, and long-term recovery. Each episode is paired with transcripts and visual summaries to make complex topics accessible. **Why It Matters** Psychosis is often misunderstood, leading to stigma, isolation, and ineffective support. This resource matters because it shifts the narrative from fear to understanding. By highlighting the importance of dignity, autonomy, and coordinated care, it helps reduce the anxiety that often accompanies a first episode.
