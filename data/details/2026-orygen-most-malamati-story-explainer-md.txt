# MOST Digital Therapy — Malamati's Story — Explainer --- ### **What Most Digital Therapy Is** Most digital therapy is a free, smartphone-based mental health support tool. It helps people manage anxiety or depression by offering: - **Personalized tools** (guided exercises, coping strategies) tailored to your needs. - **Instant access** while waiting for face-to-face therapy sessions. - A safe space to connect with others going through similar struggles. ### **3 Takeaways from Malamati’s Story** 1. **Convenience & Accessibility** Most lets users practice mental health skills anytime, anywhere—no need to wait for a therapist appointment. 2. **Tailored Support** The platform adapts to your specific issues (e.g., anxiety) with customizable activities and tools. 3. **Community & Connection** Unlike social media, Most helps you feel less alone by offering moderated peer support focused on mental health.
