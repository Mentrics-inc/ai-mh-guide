The speaker defines digital therapeutics as medical-grade software that delivers evidence-based behavioral treatments, such as cognitive behavioral therapy, via digital platforms. The discussion highlights how these tools can supplement traditional care, addressing barriers like stigma, workforce shortages, and limited access in rural areas. The presentation reviews clinical trial data demonstrating that digital interventions can improve retention in treatment and increase abstinence rates, particularly for individuals with high-risk profiles or those actively using substances. **Why It Matters** Substance use disorders affect millions, with opioid overdoses being a leading cause of death for Americans under 50. Despite effective treatments existing, up to 90% of people with SUDs do not receive care due to systemic barriers.
