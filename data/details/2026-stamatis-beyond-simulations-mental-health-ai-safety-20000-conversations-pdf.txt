This study evaluates the safety of a purpose-built mental health AI against six general-purpose large language models using both standardized benchmarks and real-world usage data. The researchers analyzed 20,000 actual user conversations and replicated four existing safety benchmarks to assess how well these systems handle high-risk topics like suicide and self-harm. The purpose-built AI model significantly reduced harmful content compared to frontier models and successfully provided crisis resources in all clinically confirmed high-risk scenarios during the real-world audit.
