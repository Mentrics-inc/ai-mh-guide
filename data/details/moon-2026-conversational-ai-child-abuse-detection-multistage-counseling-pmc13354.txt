This paper introduces an AI system designed to support child counselors by conducting conversations with children to detect signs of neglect, emotional, physical, and sexual abuse. The researchers developed and validated this tool using a large language model that generates follow-up questions, filters harmful content, and flags uncertain cases for human review. Testing on a Korean counseling dataset showed the system accurately detected abuse types and performed well in guiding conversations, with expert evaluations confirming its reliability.
