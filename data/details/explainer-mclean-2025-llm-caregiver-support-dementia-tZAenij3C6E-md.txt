The research compares responses from a specialized AI chatbot against those from an experienced human geriatric care manager to determine which provides more helpful guidance. **Why It Matters** Caregiving is a massive, often unpaid burden, primarily shouldered by women. Existing resources cannot keep up with the growing demand. This study matters because it moves beyond theoretical testing to create practical tools.
