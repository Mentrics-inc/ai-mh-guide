This study evaluates the reliability and validity of a Persian translation of the Columbia Suicide Severity Rating Scale for assessing suicide risk in Iranian soldiers. The researchers tested the adapted tool on over 600 participants, including both healthy soldiers and patients from a psychiatric hospital, using statistical methods to compare results with other established mental health questionnaires. The findings confirm that the Persian version is a reliable and accurate instrument for categorizing suicide risk levels in this population.
