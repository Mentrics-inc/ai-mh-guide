This study examines how patients and primary care providers in an urban Canadian setting understand and experience trauma-informed care. The researchers conducted semi-structured interviews with 10 patients and 13 healthcare providers to gather qualitative data on their perspectives. Participants agreed that trauma-informed care is a necessary, holistic approach for delivering quality primary care and that providers are well-positioned to integrate these practices due to the trust built in ongoing patient relationships.
