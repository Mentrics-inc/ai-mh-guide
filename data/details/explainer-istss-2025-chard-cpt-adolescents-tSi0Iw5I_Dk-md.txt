<!-- explainer from istss-2025-chard-cpt-adolescents-transcript-yt-tSi0Iw5I_Dk.txt · 9s --> **What the Content Covers** This interview features Dr. Kate Chard discussing Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT) for adolescents. CPT is an evidence-based treatment for trauma that focuses on changing unhelpful beliefs about the traumatic event, rather than requiring patients to retell the trauma story in detail. The discussion highlights how CPT differs from other therapies like Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy (TF-CBT), specifically noting that CPT makes trauma narration optional and does not require parent participation in the core sessions. It also covers practical adaptations for teens, such as using simplified worksheets and mobile apps to manage the 10–15 minutes of daily practice. **Why It Matters** Trauma can severely impact a teenager’s developing brain, affecting their ability to trust others, feel safe, or maintain focus. Traditional therapies often require extensive parental involvement or detailed trauma retelling, which can be barriers for some teens.
