This study investigates whether providing basic information about machine learning tools helps psychiatric patients trust clinical decision support systems more. Researchers conducted an online survey experiment with nearly 1,000 patients, randomly assigning them to receive specific information about the technology, standard care details, or no information at all. The results showed that patients who received information about the machine learning systems expressed significantly higher trust and lower distrust compared to those who did not.
