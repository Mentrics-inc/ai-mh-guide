# MOST: Moderated Online Social Therapy (Orygen Digital) URL: https://emhicglobal.com/case-studies/most-moderated-online-social-therapy/ ## What it is - World-first digital therapy platform blending online + face-to-face therapy for young people 15-25 (Orygen pages say 12-25 for current rollout). - Developed by Orygen (Australia's centre of excellence in youth mental health); led by Prof. Mario Alvarez-Jimenez, Director Orygen Digital. - Free in Australia (gov + philanthropic funded); rolled out Victoria-first during COVID response; other states committed; pilots in US/Canada/Europe. ## Evidence-based design - 24/7 access to personalised, evidence-based therapeutic journeys, tools, programs from any device; overseen by clinicians, peer workers, career consultants; integrates with (or bridges waitlists to) state youth MH care. - Therapy methods embedded: CBT, MBCT, CT, rumination-focused therapy, metacognitive therapy, mindfulness, self-compassion, social-cognitive interventions; journeys mapped against evidence-based clinical models. - Built by researchers, clinicians, computer scientists, creative writers, comic developers, HCI experts; co-designed with young people and families.
