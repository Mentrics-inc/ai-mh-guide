<!-- explainer from 2026-beyond-blue-future-we-choose-media-release-grey-lit.pdf --> # Understanding Australia’s Mental Health Future **What It Is** Beyond Blue and the CSIRO have released a strategic report titled *The Future We Choose*. It uses scientific foresight methods to explore how mental health in Australia might change over the next ten years. Rather than predicting one specific outcome, the report examines various possible futures based on current social, technological, and economic trends. **Why It Matters** While more people are aware of mental health issues, there is still a significant gap between those seeking help and the support available. Workforce shortages, high costs, and complex systems make accessing care difficult. Simultaneously, rapid changes in technology and society are reshaping how people experience and manage their wellbeing. This report highlights that the current trajectory—where demand outpaces supply—is not fixed.
