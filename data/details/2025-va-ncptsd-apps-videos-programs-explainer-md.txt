# VA National Center for PTSD — Apps, Videos and More Category: ptsd-trauma Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- The VA National Center for PTSD offers five categories of digital resources: ## Mobile Apps PTSD-related mobile applications providing self-help, education, and resources. Also treatment companion apps for use with a health care provider. Available for iOS and Android. Key apps include: - **PTSD Coach** — self-help, education, coping tools - **PTSD Coach Family** — support for family members - And other VA MH apps at https://mobile.va.gov/mental-health-and-behavioral-therapy-apps ## Videos Educational videos for Veterans and the General Public covering recovery, treatment, and assessment. See separate explainer for the whiteboard video series. ## PTSD Treatment Decision Aid Online tool to compare effective PTSD treatment options and think about which might be right for you. ## AboutFace Videos of Veterans, family members, and clinicians sharing their experiences with PTSD and PTSD treatment. ## Online Programs Self-help programs and courses teaching skills and tools for coping with PTSD and related problems. ## Provider Resources - Assessment tools: CAPS-5, PCL-5, etc. - Conti
