<!-- explainer from 2019-hhs-think-cultural-health-behavioral-health-training.md · 3s --> # Understanding Cultural Competency in Behavioral Health This free online training, provided by the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services, is designed for behavioral health professionals. Its primary goal is to help clinicians improve their cultural and linguistic competence. The program consists of four modules that take approximately four to five and a half hours to complete. It covers foundational concepts, self-reflection, understanding client backgrounds, and applying appropriate interventions. ### Why It Matters Cultural background significantly influences how people experience mental health, seek help, and communicate with providers. When clinicians lack cultural awareness, it can lead to misdiagnosis, poor communication, and weaker therapeutic relationships. This training addresses these gaps by teaching professionals how their own biases affect their work and how to better understand their clients’ unique perspectives.
