This paper reviews the risks and opportunities of using artificial intelligence in mental health care, focusing on the dangers of general-purpose chatbots and the potential benefits of specialized clinical tools. The author analyzes recent literature from the past five to ten years to evaluate the safety, reliability, and ethical implications of these technologies. The study finds that while AI can offer accessible support, it poses significant risks such as user dependency and inadequate crisis response, suggesting that its responsible use requires strict human oversight and specialized training.
