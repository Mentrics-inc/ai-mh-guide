This paper reviews the challenges of diagnosing borderline personality disorder in adolescents and explores how combining traditional psychological assessments with data from digital devices and artificial intelligence could improve detection. The authors analyze existing tools for measuring personality functioning alongside emerging digital indicators like speech patterns and activity data to propose a conceptual framework for future clinical use. The main finding is that while no validated hybrid system currently exists, integrating these dynamic digital measures with standard clinical evaluations offers a promising direction for more accurate and timely identification of the disorder.
