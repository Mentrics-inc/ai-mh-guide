<!-- explainer from 2025-trevor-project-lgbtq-youth-mental-health-transcript-yt-SMtZj62tN-o.txt · 9s --> **Understanding LGBTQ+ Youth Mental Health** This episode analyzes the Trevor Project’s 2024 National Survey, highlighting a mental health crisis among LGBTQ+ young people. While LGBTQ+ youth are not inherently at higher risk for suicide, societal mistreatment, discrimination, and stigma significantly increase their vulnerability. The data reveals alarming rates of depression, anxiety, and suicidal ideation, particularly among transgender, non-binary, and Indigenous youth. These findings underscore how external pressures, including anti-LGBTQ+ legislation and victimization, directly harm mental well-being. **Why It Matters** The survey shows that 39% of LGBTQ+ youth seriously considered suicide in the past year, with rates rising to 46% for transgender and non-binary individuals. Discrimination and bullying are major drivers; those who experienced victimization were more than twice as likely to attempt suicide.
