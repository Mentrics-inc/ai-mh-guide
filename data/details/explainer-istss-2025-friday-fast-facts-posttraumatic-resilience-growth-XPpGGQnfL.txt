<!-- explainer from istss-2025-friday-fast-facts-posttraumatic-resilience-growth-transcript-yt-XPpGGQnfLqE.txt · 5s --> # Understanding Post-Traumatic Growth and Resilience This video series introduces two distinct concepts in trauma recovery: resilience and post-traumatic growth. While often used interchangeably, researchers view them as separate experiences. Resilience is the most common response to adversity. It involves "bouncing back" to your previous level of functioning after a stressful event. It is about maintaining stability and returning to normalcy. Post-traumatic growth is different. It occurs when individuals experience positive psychological changes that exceed their pre-trauma baseline. This is not just about surviving; it is about thriving in new ways. Growth might look like deeper relationships with others, a renewed sense of purpose, or a shifted perspective on life. Importantly, these processes are not simple or linear. They vary significantly across different cultures, populations, and types of trauma. # Why This Matters Distinguishing between these concepts is crucial for both clinical practice and public understanding.
