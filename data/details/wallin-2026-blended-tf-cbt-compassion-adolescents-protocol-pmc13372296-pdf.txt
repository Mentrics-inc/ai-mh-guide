This paper describes the protocol for a pilot study testing a blended therapy that combines trauma-focused cognitive behavioral therapy with compassion strategies for adolescents with PTSD. The researchers will conduct a randomized controlled trial with 40 teenagers in northern Sweden, comparing the new mixed online and in-person treatment against standard care. The study is currently underway and will report results on whether the treatment is feasible and acceptable to participants by late 2028.
