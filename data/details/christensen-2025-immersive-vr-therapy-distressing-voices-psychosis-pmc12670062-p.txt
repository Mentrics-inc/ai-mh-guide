This study explores how patients and therapists experienced immersive virtual reality-assisted therapy for distressing voices in psychosis. Researchers conducted interviews with ten trial participants and eight therapists to gather qualitative data on their perspectives. The findings indicate that the therapy was generally acceptable and feasible, leading to increased patient empowerment and better dialogue with voices, though it also presented challenges such as anxiety and technical issues.
