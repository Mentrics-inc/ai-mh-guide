The study examines how mental health professionals in the United States perceive artificial intelligence and what ethical concerns they have about using it in patient care. Researchers surveyed 220 mental health workers and trainees in California and New Jersey using an online questionnaire to gather data on their awareness, attitudes, and ethical views. The results show that while participants generally view AI positively for improving care, they have significant worries about data privacy and bias, and many have not yet adopted these tools in their daily work.
