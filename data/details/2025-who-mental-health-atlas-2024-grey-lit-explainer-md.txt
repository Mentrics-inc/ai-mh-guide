<!-- explainer from 2025-who-mental-health-atlas-2024-grey-lit.pdf --> # Understanding the Mental Health Atlas 2024 The Mental Health Atlas 2024 is a comprehensive global report published by the World Health Organization (WHO). It serves as a central database that tracks the status of mental health services, policies, and resources across countries worldwide. By collecting standardized data, the Atlas allows governments, researchers, and health organizations to compare how different nations are addressing mental health challenges. ### Why It Matters Mental health is a critical component of overall public health, yet it often receives less funding and attention than physical health conditions. This document matters because it highlights significant gaps in care, such as shortages of mental health professionals and uneven access to treatment. By making this data visible, the Atlas helps identify where resources are needed most and encourages accountability among governments to improve service delivery.
