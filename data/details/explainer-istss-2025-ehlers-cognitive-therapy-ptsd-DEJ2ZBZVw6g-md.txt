<!-- explainer from istss-2025-ehlers-cognitive-therapy-ptsd-transcript-yt-DEJ2ZBZVw6g.txt · 11s --> **What This Content Covers** This interview features Dr. Anke Ehlers, developer of Cognitive Therapy for PTSD, explaining how this evidence-based treatment works. The therapy targets three core factors that keep PTSD active: disjointed trauma memories, negative personal meanings (like self-blame), and unhelpful coping strategies (such as avoidance). Unlike some treatments that rely on repetitive exposure, this approach uses an individualized case formulation to tailor interventions. Key techniques include "updating" trauma memories by integrating new, safe information, rebuilding daily life to counter feelings of hopelessness, and identifying specific sensory triggers to reduce re-experiencing symptoms. The discussion also highlights the importance of supervision for clinicians and the development of internet-delivered versions to improve accessibility. **Why It Matters** Understanding the specific mechanisms of PTSD helps explain why symptoms persist even after the traumatic event has ended.
