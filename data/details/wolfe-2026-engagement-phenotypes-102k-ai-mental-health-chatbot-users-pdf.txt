The study analyzes how different patterns of interaction with an AI mental health chatbot relate to changes in depression, anxiety, and social support among over 100,000 users. Researchers used machine learning to group users into five distinct engagement types based on their usage behavior and measured clinical outcomes through surveys and model predictions. The main finding is that while all users showed some improvement, those with high engagement levels experienced significantly greater reductions in depression and anxiety compared to those who stopped using the tool early.
