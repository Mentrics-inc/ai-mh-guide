# National Child Traumatic Stress Network (NCTSN) — Resources Overview Source: https://www.nctsn.org/resources, https://www.nctsn.org/treatments-and-practices/trauma-treatments Captured: 2026-09-12 Category: ptsd-trauma, abuse-treatment Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- The National Child Traumatic Stress Network (NCTSN) is funded by SAMHSA's Center for Mental Health Services (CMHS) and jointly coordinated by UCLA and Duke University. It is the primary US infrastructure for disseminating evidence-based child trauma interventions. ## Key Resource Areas ### Trauma Treatments (Evidence-Based Interventions) NCTSN disseminates standardized, trauma-informed clinical interventions spanning a continuum from rigorously evaluated treatments to promising/emerging practices. Core interventions include: - TF-CBT (Trauma-Focused Cognitive Behavioral Therapy) - CFTSI (Child and Family Traumatic Stress Intervention) - ARC (Attachment, Self-Regulation, and Competency) - CPP (Child-Parent Psychotherapy) - SPARCS (Structured Psychotherapy for Adolescents Responding to Chronic Stress) - BOUNCE BACK — school-based group intervention - Psychological First Aid (PFA) and Skills for Psychological Recovery (SP
