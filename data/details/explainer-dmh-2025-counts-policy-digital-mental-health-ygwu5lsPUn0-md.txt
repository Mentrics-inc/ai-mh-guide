<!-- explainer from dmh-2025-counts-policy-digital-mental-health-transcript-yt-ygwu5lsPUn0.txt · 15s --> **The Hidden Influence of Policy on Digital Mental Health** This presentation explains that digital mental health is not driven solely by technology or market forces, but is fundamentally shaped by government policy. Policy determines who gets funding, which innovations reach the market, and who can afford to use them. Without intentional policy changes, the current system often favors large corporations and incremental improvements rather than breakthrough solutions that address widespread mental health crises, such as rising suicide rates among adolescents. Understanding this landscape is crucial for advocates and developers who want to create meaningful, equitable impact. **Why This Matters** Current policy frameworks often create invisible barriers that limit innovation and equity. For example, complex regulations like FDA oversight and HIPAA privacy laws can discourage smaller innovators while benefiting large incumbents with resources to navigate bureaucracy.
