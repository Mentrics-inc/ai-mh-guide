Experts Professor Neil Greenberg and Professor David Forbes examine whether routine screening helps or harms workforce health. They highlight that while screening is widely used, current evidence suggests it often fails to improve mental health outcomes or reduce sickness absence. The discussion emphasizes that screening is not a standalone solution but must be part of a broader system involving support, culture, and accessible care. # Why This Matters Relying on mandatory screening can create false security or unnecessary stigma. Studies show that pre-screening often incorrectly excludes capable workers, while post-deployment screening frequently fails to detect true needs because workers fear career consequences.
