This study investigates whether individuals with Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder experience unhelpful thoughts and beliefs similar to those found in Anorexia Nervosa. The researchers compared 184 adults with Anorexia Nervosa, Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder, or no eating disorder using online surveys to assess their core beliefs and weight-related concerns. The main finding is that people with Avoidant/Restrictive Food Intake Disorder have significantly more of these unhelpful thoughts than those without an eating disorder, and these thoughts help explain their efforts to prevent weight gain.
