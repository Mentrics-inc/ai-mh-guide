<!-- explainer from 2025-va-cpt-coach-app-intro-transcript-yt-CE2XGZZeidE.txt · 5s --> # Understanding CPT Coach for PTSD CPT Coach is a free, secure mobile application designed to support individuals undergoing Cognitive Processing Therapy (CPT) for posttraumatic stress disorder (PTSD). Developed by the Department of Veterans Affairs, this tool serves as a digital companion to traditional therapy. It does not replace professional care but helps patients engage more deeply with their treatment between sessions. The app focuses on helping users identify and modify unhelpful thought patterns that hinder recovery, making the therapeutic process more organized and accessible. # Why This Matters PTSD recovery often requires consistent effort and reflection outside of clinical settings. Many patients struggle to keep track of worksheets, monitor their emotional progress, or remember specific therapeutic concepts. CPT Coach addresses these challenges by centralizing key components of CPT into one easy-to-use platform.
