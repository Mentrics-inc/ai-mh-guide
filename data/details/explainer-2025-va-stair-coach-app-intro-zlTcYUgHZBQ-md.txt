<!-- explainer from 2025-va-stair-coach-app-intro-transcript-yt-zlTcYUgHZBQ.txt · 4s --> # Understanding STAIR Coach STAIR Coach is a free, secure mobile application developed by the Department of Veterans Affairs to help people manage the emotional and relational challenges that often follow trauma. The app is based on an evidence-based therapy called STAIR, which focuses on teaching skills for regulating emotions and improving interpersonal relationships. While the app is designed for public use, it is most effective when used alongside a trained mental health professional. # Why It Matters Trauma can significantly disrupt how individuals process feelings and connect with others. By providing structured, accessible tools, STAIR Coach empowers users to take an active role in their healing journey. It bridges the gap between clinical therapy and daily life, offering consistent support that helps users build resilience, understand their emotional patterns, and develop healthier coping mechanisms.
