<!-- explainer from 2026-beyondblue-future-we-choose-media-release-grey-lit.pdf --> # Understanding Australia’s Mental Health Future **What It Is** Beyond Blue and the CSIRO have released a strategic report titled *The Future We Choose*. Rather than predicting a single outcome, the report uses scientific foresight to explore how mental health in Australia might change over the next ten years. It examines how social, technological, and economic shifts will impact wellbeing and identifies different possible futures based on the choices made today. **Why It Matters** While awareness of mental health is improving, demand for support currently outpaces available resources. The report highlights that traditional systems often react to crises rather than preventing them. With rapid changes in technology and community structures, there is a risk that support systems could become stretched or less trusted. Understanding these trends is crucial for building a system that is preventative, accessible, and resilient for future generations. **3 Key Takeaways** 1. **The Future Is Not Fixed:** Current trends do not guarantee a negative outcome.
