 TITLE: PTSD treatment: "My sons tell me I'm a lot easier to live with now." Veteran Craig "Stu" Shipley URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JbTOJ9xQ6rQ My sons feel that I have taken the right step and they tell me that I'm a lot easier to live with now. [laughs] And I don't jump off handle like I used to years ago. They said, "You've mellowed." I'm the same person I was 20 years ago, but I'm in control of this person. I have a better understanding of who I am, what I saw, what I went through, along with the other men that laid it down for me. And it's a good feeling.
