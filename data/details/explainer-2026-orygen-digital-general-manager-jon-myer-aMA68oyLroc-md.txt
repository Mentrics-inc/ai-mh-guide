 TITLE: Explainer 2026 Orygen Digital General Manager Jon Myer Ama68Oylroc.Md URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aMA68oyLroc <!-- explainer from 2026-orygen-digital-general-manager-jon-myer-transcript-yt-aMA68oyLroc.txt · 6s --> # Enhancing Youth Mental Health Through Technology Orygen Digital is a division dedicated to improving mental health services for young people by leveraging modern technology. The organization operates in response to an outdated healthcare system identified by the Royal Commission into Youth Mental Health. Their mission is to build, innovate, and scale digital solutions that reach young people where they are. Currently, they are expanding their footprint across Australian states, including Victoria, New South Wales, Queensland, and the ACT, supported by government bodies and philanthropic partners. The centerpiece of their current work is MOST, a moderated online social therapy platform. This mobile-optimized tool serves as a comprehensive resource, offering therapy modules, a safe social network for peer support, and access to professionals like therapists and career consultants.
