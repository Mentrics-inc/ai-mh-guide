<!-- explainer from dmh-2025-gazzaley-experiential-medicine-cognitive-enhancement-transcript-yt-xt-9eAfGTR0.txt · 14s --> # Understanding Digital Therapeutics for Brain Health This interview with Dr. Adam Gazzaley, a neuroscientist and entrepreneur, explores the emerging field of using video games as medical treatments. Dr. Gazzaley explains how he transitioned from studying brain plasticity to creating interactive digital tools that improve cognitive function. He argues that while technology has contributed to a "cognition crisis" by increasing distraction, it can also be the solution. His work focuses on "closed-loop" experiences, where games adapt in real-time to the user’s performance to challenge the brain and drive positive neural changes. # Why This Matters The discussion highlights a shift from viewing games solely as entertainment to recognizing them as valid medical interventions. Dr. Gazzaley’s company, Akili Interactive, developed *Endeavor RX*, the first video game cleared by the FDA for treating ADHD in children.
