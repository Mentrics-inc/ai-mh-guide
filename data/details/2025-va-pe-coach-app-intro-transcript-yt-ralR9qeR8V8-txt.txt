 TITLE: 2025 Va Pe Coach App Intro URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ralR9qeR8V8 Graphic: VA Logo Narrator: Prolonged Exposure - or PE - Therapy is an evidence-based psychotherapy used to treat posttraumatic stress disorder, or PTSD. By participating in PE with your therapist’s guidance, you can gradually decrease your PTSD symptoms. If you are in PE Therapy with a mental health provider, the PE Coach App can be a useful companion to your care. In this video, you’ll learn how to use the PE Coach app alongside your therapist. The first thing you’ll do in the PE Coach app is “Add a Session.” Here you’ll add information about your upcoming PE session. Enter the date and time of your first session with your therapist. If you like, you can also add the appointment to your device’s calendar. Use the app to set a reminder. You’ll get alerts on your smartphone to remind you of upcoming appointments. After you’ve added your first session, you will see a list of tasks to complete. Under “Review Assessment Settings,” select the assessments your therapist has recommended.
