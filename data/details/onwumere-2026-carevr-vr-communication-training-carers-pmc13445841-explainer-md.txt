Psychosis can involve distressing symptoms like hearing voices or holding unusual beliefs, which often make communication difficult and stressful for both the patient and their carers. This study explored whether CareVR could effectively teach carers better ways to interact during these challenging moments. # Why It Matters Effective communication is crucial for the wellbeing of both the person experiencing psychosis and their caregiver. Poor interactions can lead to higher relapse rates, increased hospital visits, and greater strain on the caregiving relationship. Traditional support methods often lack real-time practice opportunities.
