<!-- explainer from dmh-2025-little-treatments-big-effects-schleider-transcript-yt-Q6oLh6Y_mZY.txt · 20s --> **The Mental Health Access Gap** This webinar discusses the critical shortage of mental health providers and the fact that most young people who need care never receive it. Even when youth access treatment, they typically attend only a few sessions, far fewer than the 12 to 16 sessions standard therapies require to be effective. Traditional models often fail because they rely on parents as gatekeepers and assume long-term commitment, which many teens cannot or will not provide. **Single-Session Interventions (SSIs)** The speaker introduces Single-Session Interventions (SSIs) as a scalable solution. These are brief, structured programs—often digital and self-guided—that deliver meaningful support in one encounter. Unlike traditional therapy, SSIs do not assume the user will return for more. Instead, they aim to create immediate shifts in hopelessness or agency, empowering users to believe change is possible right now.
