The discussion outlines how TF-CBT helps children and adolescents recover from various traumas, including abuse, violence, and disasters. Dr. Deblinger explains the therapy’s three-phase structure: building safety and coping skills, processing the traumatic memory, and consolidating new skills. She highlights the "PRACTICE" components, which range from psychoeducation and relaxation techniques to narrative processing. A key focus is the active involvement of caregivers, who receive support to manage their own stress and improve parenting skills.
