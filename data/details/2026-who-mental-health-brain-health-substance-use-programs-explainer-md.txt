# WHO — Mental Health, Brain Health and Substance Use Programs Category: psychiatry-general Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- WHO's Mental Health, Brain Health and Substance Use team works on promotion, prevention, and scaling up care for MNS (mental, neurological, substance use) disorders worldwide. This page indexes their major programs and publications. ## Key Programs ### mhGAP (Mental Health Gap Action Programme) - Launched 2008 to address the treatment gap for MNS disorders - **mhGAP Intervention Guide v2.0 (2016)**: evidence-based guidance for non-specialist health workers in LMICs - **mhGAP Guideline 2023/2025 update**: 30 updated + 18 new + 90 pre-existing recommendations. Third iteration, reflecting 15 years of investment. - Covers: depression, psychosis, epilepsy, developmental disorders, behavioral disorders, dementia, substance use, suicide, self-harm, stress, PTSD - Free download: https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/9789240084278 ### Comprehensive Mental Health Action Plan 2013-2030 - Framework for all WHO member states - Targets: 20% increase in service coverage, 20% reduction in suicide rates ### Key Publications - **World Mental Health Report 2022**: "Transforming
