This narrative review and conceptual perspective explores the potential integra- tion of expressive writing (EW) and digital cognitive therapy (DCT) as a proposed dual approach to support emotional regulation and cognitive recovery. EW can facilitate emotional processing and motivation, while digital therapy may improve attention, memory, and executive function through structured and interactive training.
