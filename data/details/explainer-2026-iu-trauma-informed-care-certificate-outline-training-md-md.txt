<!-- explainer from 2026-iu-trauma-informed-care-certificate-outline-training.md · 3s --> # What This Course Covers This free, self-paced online certificate from Indiana University introduces Trauma-Informed Care (TIC). It is designed for anyone working with or supporting others, including those in rural areas, youth, Veterans, and people with disabilities. The curriculum consists of two main parts: a four-hour foundation module explaining the core concepts of trauma, and a two-hour specialized module focusing on a specific population. The course uses videos, animations, and interviews to teach that trauma is widespread and affects how people think and behave. It distinguishes between general stress, adverse childhood experiences, and clinical PTSD, while explaining how trauma physically impacts the brain. # Why It Matters Understanding trauma is crucial because it changes how individuals respond to their environment. Without this knowledge, well-meaning helpers might inadvertently re-traumatize people by using rigid, controlling, or unsupportive approaches.
