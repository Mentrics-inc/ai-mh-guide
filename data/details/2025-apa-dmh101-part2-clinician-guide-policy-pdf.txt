This paper provides an introduction to mental health mobile applications for clinicians and patients, outlining their use across various care settings. It reviews the application of these tools in outpatient, inpatient, telepsychiatry, integrated care, and child psychiatry contexts to address treatment gaps and accessibility issues. The main finding is that while digital mental health tools show promise for improving care access and outcomes, their adoption remains low due to barriers such as privacy concerns, workflow integration challenges, and a lack of clinician recommendations.
