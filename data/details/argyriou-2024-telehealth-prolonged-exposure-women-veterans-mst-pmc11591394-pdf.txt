This study aims to determine the best way to assign women veterans with military sexual trauma to either in-person or online therapy for PTSD based on their personal circumstances. The researchers used a machine learning technique to create a personalized rule that considers factors like age, race, and location to decide which treatment format is most effective for each individual. The results show that this personalized approach significantly reduces PTSD symptoms compared to assigning everyone to online therapy, but it does not clearly favor in-person visits over online ones.
