This study develops and validates a new survey tool to measure how trauma is passed from refugee parents to their children. Researchers tested this tool with two groups of Lao American young adults, totaling 312 participants. The results show the tool effectively identifies five specific ways trauma is communicated, such as through silence or cultural sharing, and these pathways are linked to higher levels of stress and depression in the young adults.
