The paper describes the creation of a digital platform designed to deliver culturally adapted family therapy for psychosis among people of sub-Saharan African and Caribbean descent. The researchers used an iterative co-design process involving workshops and interviews with service users and therapists to develop and refine the website. This approach resulted in a user-friendly, accessible digital tool that addresses healthcare inequalities by providing a culturally relevant alternative to traditional in-person therapy.
