This study examined how posttraumatic stress symptoms and mental well-being relate to how actively users engage with a mobile app designed to help veterans cope with military sexual trauma. Researchers analyzed anonymous usage data from over 27,000 app users, focusing on those who completed specific mental health assessments within the app. The main finding was that users with moderate symptom levels engaged more with the app than those with very low or very high symptoms, while higher mental well-being was generally linked to increased engagement.
