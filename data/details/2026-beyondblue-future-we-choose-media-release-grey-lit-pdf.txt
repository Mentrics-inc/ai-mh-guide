This report explores how Australia’s mental health system might change over the next ten years and what choices can shape a better future. Researchers used strategic foresight methods, including horizon scanning and scenario planning, to analyze social, technological, and economic trends affecting mental wellbeing. The study finds that while current trends point toward strained resources and low trust, the future is not fixed and can be improved through preventive care, stronger community connections, and trusted technology.
