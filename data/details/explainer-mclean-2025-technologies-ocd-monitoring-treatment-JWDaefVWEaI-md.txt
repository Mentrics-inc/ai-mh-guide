<!-- explainer from mclean-2025-technologies-ocd-monitoring-treatment-transcript-yt-JWDaefVWEaI.txt · 22s --> **Symposium on New OCD Technologies** This symposium explores emerging technologies designed to improve the monitoring and treatment of Obsessive-Compulsive Disorder (OCD). Speakers highlighted the severity of OCD, noting it affects 1–3% of the global population and carries a significant risk of suicide. The discussions focused on using advanced tools to better understand brain activity, predict treatment outcomes, and support patients during therapy. **Why It Matters** OCD symptoms can consume up to 12 hours a day, severely impairing quality of life. Traditional treatments like Exposure and Response Prevention (ERP) are effective but require high patient adherence. Many patients drop out early, leading to poor outcomes. New technologies offer ways to identify these risks early and provide personalized support.
