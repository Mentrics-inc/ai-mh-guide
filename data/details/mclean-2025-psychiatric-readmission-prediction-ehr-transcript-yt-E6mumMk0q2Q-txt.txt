Unplanned psychiatric readmission present a major challenge for patients, families, and health care systems. Individual with mood and psychotic disorders face some of the highest 30-day readmission rates, driving poor outcome and higher cost. Yet identifying those most at risk remains remarkably difficult. Advances in natural language processing based machine learning offer a promising path forward. By applying these computational approaches to electronic health records, we can develop robust scalable algorithms for readmission risk prediction. This symposium brings together three complimentary perspectives on this emerging science focusing on structural and unstructured EHR data and the large language model. Let me introduce our speakers.
