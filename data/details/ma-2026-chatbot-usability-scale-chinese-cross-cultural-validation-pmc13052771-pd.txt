BackgroundChatbots are increasingly deployed across various domains; however, systematic evaluation of their usability remains limited, particularly in non-Western contexts. The 11-item Chatbot Usability Scale (BUS-11), a multidimensional instrument grounded in human-computer interaction theory, has demonstrated strong psychometric properties in prior studies; however, a validated Chinese version does not exist, despite China being one of the largest chatbot markets.ObjectiveThis study aimed to translate, culturally adapt, and validate the BUS-11 for Chinese users.MethodsFollowing established cross-cultural adaptation procedures, the scale was forward- and back-translated, reviewed by an expert committee, and pilot-tested for clarity and feasibility.
