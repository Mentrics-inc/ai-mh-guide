This study examines the prevalence and risk factors for suicidal thoughts among unaccompanied young refugees. The researchers used machine learning algorithms to analyze data from over 700 individuals, focusing on factors like trauma symptoms, depression, and family status. The main finding was that past suicide attempts, trauma symptoms, and depression are key risk factors, while having a living mother is protective, allowing the model to accurately identify at-risk youth.
