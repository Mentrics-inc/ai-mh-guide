This paper evaluates whether machine learning models used to predict patient aggression in psychiatric hospitals show bias against specific demographic groups. The researchers trained an algorithm on electronic health records from over 17,000 patients and measured prediction errors across different racial, gender, and socioeconomic subgroups. They found that the model produced significantly more false alarms for Black and Middle Eastern patients, men, and those admitted by police or living in unstable housing, indicating that the tool could reinforce existing social inequalities.
