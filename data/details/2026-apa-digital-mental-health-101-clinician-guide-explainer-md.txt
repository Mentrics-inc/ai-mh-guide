# APA — Digital Mental Health: Clinician Guide Category: psychiatry-general Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- Nearly half of the world's mental health patients lack access to treatment, but well over half of those diagnosed with a mental illness have access to a smartphone. This fact highlights the paradigm shift that psychiatry is poised to lead around mobile health (mHealth). ## Key Content ### Definition APA focuses on asynchronous mobile technologies: smartphone apps, text messaging, email, and online forums.
