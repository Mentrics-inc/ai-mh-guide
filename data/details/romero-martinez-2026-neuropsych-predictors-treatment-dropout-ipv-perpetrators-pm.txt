This study investigates whether specific cognitive abilities predict which men convicted of intimate partner violence will drop out of court-mandated treatment programs. Researchers tested 309 participants using a series of standardized psychological assessments to measure intelligence, planning skills, attention, and mental flexibility. The results showed that individuals with lower nonverbal intelligence, poorer planning abilities, and more difficulty sustaining attention were significantly more likely to leave the program early.
