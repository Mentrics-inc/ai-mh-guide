<!-- explainer from mclean-2025-digital-navigators-mental-health-transcript-yt-5bNPDRy_Qb4.txt · 21s --> **What This Session Covers** This presentation explores the emerging role of "digital navigators" in healthcare. These are trained staff members who help patients overcome barriers to using digital health tools, such as apps or online portals. The speakers share how they train these navigators to bridge the "digital divide"—the gap between those who have access to technology and those who do not. They detail a four-part training curriculum that covers technical troubleshooting, digital literacy education, matching patients with appropriate health tools, and using motivational interviewing to encourage patient engagement. The session also highlights real-world implementations in the US, Canada, and Germany, showing how navigators are integrated into clinics, research settings, and community centers. **Why It Matters** Digital health offers significant benefits, but its potential is limited if patients cannot or will not use the technology.
