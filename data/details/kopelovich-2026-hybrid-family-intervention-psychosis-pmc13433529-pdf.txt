This study evaluates a hybrid digital training program designed to help family caregivers of individuals with psychosis manage their loved one's condition. The researchers delivered the intervention to 275 caregivers through a combination of online courses, live virtual workshops, and coaching from trained family peers. Participants showed significant improvements in reducing their own stress and negative attitudes while gaining greater confidence in using supportive skills four months after the training.
