This study compares traditional statistical methods with machine learning algorithms to predict depression and identify key contributing factors. The researchers analyzed data from the World Health Organization’s Study on Global AGEing and Adult Health in India, testing ten different predictive models. The main finding is that while machine learning models like Random Forest and Ridge regression offered slightly better accuracy, they all showed only moderate ability to distinguish between individuals with and without depression.
