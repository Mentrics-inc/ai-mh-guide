# NICE Recommends 8 Digitally Enabled Therapies for Depression and Anxiety Category: psychotherapy Type: policy (SRC: web) --- Eight digitally enabled therapies to treat depression and anxiety disorders in adults have been conditionally recommended by NICE in draft guidance (March 2023). ## The 8 Therapies ### For anxiety disorders (6 technologies): 1. **Perspectives** — for body dysmorphic disorder (BDD); support from a high intensity therapist trained in treating BDD. 2. **Beating the Blues** — for generalised anxiety symptoms or unspecified anxiety disorder; support from a psychological wellbeing practitioner or high intensity therapist. 3. **Space from Anxiety (SilverCloud)** — for generalised anxiety symptoms or unspecified anxiety disorder; same support model as Beating the Blues. 4. **iCT-PTSD** — for PTSD; support from a high intensity therapist trained in treating PTSD. 5. **Spring** — for PTSD; same support model as iCT-PTSD. 6. **iCT-SAD** — for social anxiety disorder; support from a high intensity therapist trained in treating social anxiety disorder. ### For depression (3 technologies, overlapping with above): 7. **Beating the Blues** — for depression (also listed abo
