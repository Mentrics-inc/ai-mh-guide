Sarah Bendle, a clinical psychologist, explains that trauma—often resulting from abuse—is a major factor in mental health issues but has historically been overlooked in psychosis care. Past approaches frequently ignored these experiences because clinicians lacked training or feared causing distress. Consequently, many young people felt misunderstood by a system that seemed dismissive of their painful histories. # Why This Matters Ignoring trauma leaves a significant gap in effective treatment. When services fail to address the root causes of distress, young people may feel invalidated, hindering their recovery. The research from Origins Research demonstrates that trauma is central to early psychosis, yet care models have lagged behind this evidence. By failing to address trauma, traditional methods may inadvertently cause further harm or fail to provide meaningful support.
