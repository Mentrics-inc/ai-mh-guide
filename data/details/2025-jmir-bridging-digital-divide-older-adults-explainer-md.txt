# JMIR Blog — Bridging the Digital Divide for Older Adults Category: special-populations Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- Scoping review by Dr. Sandra Just (UiT The Arctic University of Norway) and Jennifer Stanford (Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin) published in JMIR Mental Health, exploring digitalized treatment options for older adults with mental illness. ## Key Findings 1. **Low study quality**: Overall quality of included studies was low, making firm conclusions difficult. 2. **Limited modern technology research**: Only 7% of studies examined AI or VR — significant gap for cutting-edge tools in this population. 3. **Focus on dementia**: 58% of research focused on dementia, leaving major gaps for other mental disorders in older adults. 4. **The "digital gray divide"**: Existing research doesn't provide a clear roadmap for bridging the gap in technology access and use among older adults. 5. **Ethical considerations**: Limited discussion of data privacy, data security, and the importance of maintaining human connection in digital contexts. ## Positive example found A smart grower app that enables people with dementia to care for plants at home, increasing their happiness. ## R
