 TITLE: 2025 Jmir Bridging Digital Divide Older Adults Explainer.Md URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ts-explaine # JMIR Blog — Bridging the Digital Divide for Older Adults Source: https://blog.jmir.org/bridging-the-digital-divide-for-older-adults Captured: 2026-09-12 Category: special-populations Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- Scoping review by Dr. Sandra Just (UiT The Arctic University of Norway) and Jennifer Stanford (Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin) published in JMIR Mental Health, exploring digitalized treatment options for older adults with mental illness. ## Key Findings 1. **Low study quality**: Overall quality of included studies was low, making firm conclusions difficult. 2. **Limited modern technology research**: Only 7% of studies examined AI or VR — significant gap for cutting-edge tools in this population. 3. **Focus on dementia**: 58% of research focused on dementia, leaving major gaps for other mental disorders in older adults. 4. **The "digital gray divide"**: Existing research doesn't provide a clear roadmap for bridging the gap in technology access and use among older adults. 5. **Ethical considerations**: Limited discussion of data privacy, data security
