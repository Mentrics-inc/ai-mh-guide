# NIMH — Depression Overview Category: depression-anxiety Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- Depression (major depressive disorder / clinical depression) causes severe symptoms affecting how you feel, think, and handle daily activities. It can affect anyone regardless of age, race, income, culture, or education. Genetic, biological, environmental, and psychological factors all play a role. ## Key Points - One of the most common mental disorders in the US - Symptoms range from mild to severe, disrupting daily activities - Linked to increased health care costs and other conditions (heart disease, diabetes) - Risk factor for suicidal thoughts and behaviors ## NIMH Research Directions - Improving existing treatments: medications, talk therapy (including telehealth and online options), brain stimulation therapy - Identifying which treatment works best for a specific person (precision medicine) - Developing therapies for treatment-resistant depression - New methods to prevent and treat depression - Improving delivery of care for people living with depression ## Federal Resources - Depression (MedlinePlus) - Get Your Teen Screened for Depression (ODPHP) - Moms' Mental Health Matters: Depressi
