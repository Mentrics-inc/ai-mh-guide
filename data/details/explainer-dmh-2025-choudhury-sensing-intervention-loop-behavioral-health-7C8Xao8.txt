<!-- explainer from dmh-2025-choudhury-sensing-intervention-loop-behavioral-health-transcript-yt-7C8Xao8X1uI.txt · 13s --> **The Rise of Digital Mental Health Sensing** This presentation explores how digital tools, such as smartphones and wearables, can continuously monitor behavioral changes to improve mental health care. The speaker argues that traditional, sporadic clinical assessments often fail to capture the full picture of a patient’s well-being. By leveraging passive data collection, researchers can detect subtle shifts in sleep, activity, and social interaction that precede mental health declines. The core goal is to create a "sensing-intervention loop," where real-time data triggers timely, personalized support, making care more scalable and cost-effective. **Why It Matters** Mental health comorbidity with chronic illnesses leads to worse outcomes and higher costs. Current diagnostic methods are often inconsistent and rely on self-reporting, which can be unreliable. With increased acceptance of remote monitoring post-pandemic, there is a unique opportunity to integrate technology into routine care.
