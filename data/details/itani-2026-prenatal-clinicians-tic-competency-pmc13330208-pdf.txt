This paper explores how prenatal healthcare providers perceive their ability to deliver trauma-informed care and their experiences with related training. The researchers conducted semi-structured interviews with 23 clinicians from various professional backgrounds to gather qualitative and quantitative data. The study found that while providers support trauma-informed care, their current training is fragmented and insufficient, creating a gap between their knowledge and actual clinical practice.
