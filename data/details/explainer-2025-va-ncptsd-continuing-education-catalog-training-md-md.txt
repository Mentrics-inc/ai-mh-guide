<!-- explainer from 2025-va-ncptsd-continuing-education-catalog-training.md · 4s --> # Understanding Free PTSD Training Resources The VA National Center for PTSD offers a comprehensive catalog of free continuing education (CE) courses for healthcare providers. This resource is considered the most extensive free training library on Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) in the United States. It covers trauma assessment, evidence-based treatments, and specialized care for diverse populations. ### Why It Matters Access to high-quality, free training helps ensure that clinicians are up-to-date with the latest research and best practices. This directly impacts patient care by promoting standardized, effective treatments. The catalog addresses critical gaps in care, such as cultural competence, suicide prevention, and treating co-occurring conditions like substance use or chronic pain.
