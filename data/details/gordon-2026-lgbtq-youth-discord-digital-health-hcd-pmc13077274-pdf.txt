This study explores how to involve LGBTQ+ youth in designing a digital health tool to improve HIV testing rates. Researchers used the social media platform Discord to engage 21 young people in co-designing and refining the intervention over nine months. The findings show that Discord is an effective and acceptable platform for this process because its privacy and community features create a safe space for open communication and meaningful participation.
