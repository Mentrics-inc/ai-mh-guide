This study aimed to determine if a smartphone app could accurately predict both the diagnosis of depression and the response to treatment in adolescents. Researchers collected passive data on smartphone usage and physical movement from 24 depressed teens and 10 healthy peers, then used deep learning algorithms to analyze these digital patterns. The results showed that the system could predict depression diagnosis with 77% accuracy and treatment response with 76% accuracy, identifying specific smartphone behaviors as key indicators for each.
