Developed by a global panel of experts, it moves beyond traditional mental health models by focusing on immediate, practical support rather than clinical diagnosis. The model validates what workers are already doing and provides a common language to discuss stress without stigma. # Why It Matters In high-pressure environments, stress often leads to burnout, isolation, and decreased performance. This model matters because it normalizes stress reactions, acknowledging that everyone cycles through different levels of distress. By providing a structured yet adaptable approach, it helps individuals recognize early warning signs in themselves and colleagues.
