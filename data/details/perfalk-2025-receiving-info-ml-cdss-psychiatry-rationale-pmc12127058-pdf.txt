This study investigates whether providing healthcare staff with basic information about machine learning-based clinical decision support systems increases their trust in these tools. The researchers conducted a randomized survey experiment involving 780 psychiatric service employees in the Central Denmark Region who were assigned to receive different types of information or no information at all. The main finding was that staff members who received information about the systems showed significantly higher trust and lower distrust compared to those who did not receive such information.
