<!-- explainer from istss-2025-opening-access-treat-ptsd-primary-care-transcript-yt-50371xzLld4.txt · 14s --> **Understanding Trauma Care in Primary Settings** This webinar explores how to effectively treat Post-Traumatic Stress Disorder (PTSD) within primary care clinics, rather than relying solely on specialized mental health centers. Dr. Sheila Rous explains that while most people experience trauma, few seek or receive traditional therapy due to access barriers or stigma. To address this, she introduces "Prolonged Exposure in Primary Care" (PEPC), a brief, structured intervention designed for general practitioners and embedded behavioral health providers. **Why It Matters** PTSD is prevalent and often co-occurs with other medical issues like heart disease and chronic pain. Specialty mental health systems are overwhelmed and cannot meet the global demand. By integrating evidence-based treatments into primary care, patients can receive immediate, accessible support where they already seek medical attention.
