While it does not cover every possible trauma, such as peer bullying or community violence, it provides a crucial snapshot of early adversity. Before this study, most people viewed childhood violence merely as a cause for immediate physical injury. The ACE study changed this perspective by revealing that early trauma is deeply linked to serious adult health issues, including diabetes, high blood pressure, mental illness, and substance use. ### Why This Matters Recognizing the link between childhood stress and adult disease is essential for public health. It shifts the focus from treating symptoms to preventing the root causes of chronic illness.
