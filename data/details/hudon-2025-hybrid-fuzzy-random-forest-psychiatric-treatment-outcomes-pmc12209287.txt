This study develops an interpretable computer model to predict whether a court will approve a psychiatric treatment order for a patient who lacks the capacity to consent. The researchers analyzed 176 legal judgments from Quebec in 2024 using a hybrid system that combines fuzzy logic with a machine learning algorithm called Random Forest. The model achieved very high accuracy in predicting outcomes, identifying that the length of the requested treatment, the patient's age, and their compliance history were the most important factors in the decision.
