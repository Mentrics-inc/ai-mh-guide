She explains that CPT helps individuals identify and change unhelpful thoughts related to trauma, such as self-blame or beliefs that the world is entirely dangerous. The goal is to help patients become their own therapists by learning to evaluate evidence and adjust their thinking. The discussion also covers how CPT is implemented, including the shift from rigid 12-session protocols to flexible, personalized lengths of treatment. Additionally, it highlights the use of telehealth, group formats, and self-help books to improve access. **Why It Matters** Trauma affects diverse populations, including military personnel, civilians, and individuals across different cultures. Effective, accessible treatment is crucial because traditional therapy models often struggle with high dropout rates and limited availability.
