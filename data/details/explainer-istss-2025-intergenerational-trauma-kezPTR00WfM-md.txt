<!-- explainer from istss-2025-intergenerational-trauma-transcript-yt-kezPTR00WfM.txt · 12s --> # Understanding Intergenerational Trauma **What It Covers** This presentation explores how trauma experienced by parents before their children are born can affect the next generation’s mental health and brain development. Dr. Tanya Jovanovic discusses research showing that childhood trauma, such as violence or abuse, creates lasting biological and behavioral changes. These changes can influence how a child’s brain processes fear and stress, even if the child never experienced the original traumatic event themselves. The talk highlights both the risks of these effects and the protective power of supportive parenting. **Why It Matters** Trauma is a global issue affecting millions of children and adults. Understanding intergenerational transmission helps explain why mental health challenges like anxiety and PTSD can persist across families. Recognizing these patterns is crucial for developing better treatments and interventions.
