# The Trevor Project — Research Program Overview Category: minority-populations Type: explainer (SRC: web) --- The Trevor Project conducts research to save LGBTQ+ lives and reveal problems that need solving. Key statistics and research initiatives: ## 2025 National Survey on LGBTQ+ Youth Mental Health - 36% of LGBTQ+ young people seriously considered attempting suicide in the past year — including 40% of transgender and nonbinary young people - 90% said recent anti-LGBTQ+ laws, policies, and debates caused them stress or anxiety - 44% who wanted mental health care in the past year were not able to get it ## Project SPARK (Studying Protective And Risk Factors) Longitudinal study following 1,689 LGBTQ+ youth ages 13-24 across the US: - 47% experienced suicidal ideation (up from 41% a year ago) - 60% who wanted mental health care had access to it (down from 80% a year ago) - 62% reduced odds of suicidal ideation with high family support ## Recent Research Briefs (2026) - School Experiences of Trans/Nonbinary/Gender-Expansive Youth (Aug 2026): 56% reported attending a school with at least one discriminatory policy; 97% had at least one supportive educator - Intersectionality of Discrim
