This study explores the development and initial testing of an AI chatbot designed to support patients recovering from substance use disorders. Researchers conducted a small pilot study involving focus groups, usability testing with patients, and the creation of both rule-based and advanced AI chatbots. The results showed that the chatbot was easy to use and well-received by participants, who appreciated its availability and nonjudgmental nature, although the team noted that larger studies are needed to measure its actual impact on recovery.
