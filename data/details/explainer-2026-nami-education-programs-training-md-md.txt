<!-- explainer from 2026-nami-education-programs-training.md · 3s --> # Understanding NAMI’s Free Mental Health Resources The National Alliance on Mental Illness (NAMI) provides a wide array of free education, skills training, and support programs across the United States. Delivered by trained volunteers who often have lived experience with mental health challenges, these resources aim to bridge the gap between clinical care and community support. The content covers diverse needs, including specific classes for parents of youth, military families, and individuals managing their own conditions. Additionally, NAMI offers presentations for schools and communities, focusing on awareness, stigma reduction, and crisis communication. This matters because mental health literacy is essential for early intervention and effective support. NAMI’s programs are the most widely available non-expert mental health education in the country. They provide crucial tools for understanding diagnoses, coping with stress, and advocating for better care.
