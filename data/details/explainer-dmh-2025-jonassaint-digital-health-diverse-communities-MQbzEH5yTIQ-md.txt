<!-- explainer from dmh-2025-jonassaint-digital-health-diverse-communities-transcript-yt-MQbzEH5yTIQ.txt · 15s --> **Understanding Digital Mental Health for Sickle Cell Disease** This presentation explores how digital cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT) can help manage pain and mental health in people with sickle cell disease. The speaker highlights that stress and pain are closely linked, often creating a vicious cycle where distress worsens physical symptoms. Traditional in-person therapy is often inaccessible due to cost, location, and a lack of culturally competent providers. To address this, researchers developed a digital CBT program tailored specifically for African American communities, who have historically been underrepresented in such studies. **Why It Matters** Sickle cell disease causes severe, frequent pain crises and significantly reduces life expectancy. Many patients face high rates of hospitalization due to poor pain management and psychosocial stressors like discrimination and low social support.
