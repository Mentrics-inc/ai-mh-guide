This study explores how migrant women in Australia experience and navigate mental health support during pregnancy and the postpartum period. Researchers conducted online focus groups and interviews with women from Chinese, Arabic, and Indian language backgrounds to understand their interactions with clinical, community, and digital care systems. The findings reveal that while digital tools can help women seek support, they are often hindered by language barriers, limited digital skills, and a lack of cultural relevance in existing services.
