The speaker highlights that while up to 10% of people will experience eating disorder symptoms, fewer than 20% receive evidence-based care. A major barrier is that many individuals do not recognize their symptoms or know how to find help. To address this, the team developed rule-based chatbots designed to screen for risk, provide prevention education, and guide users toward professional treatment. These tools aim to bridge the gap between initial screening and actual care, particularly for those who have never sought help before. **Why It Matters** Eating disorders carry high risks of medical complications, poor quality of life, and mortality.
