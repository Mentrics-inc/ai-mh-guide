Today we're kicking off a new series exploring post-traumatic growth and resilience, two of the many facets of post-traumatic recovery. Although considered conceptually similar, re research distinguishes post-traumatic growth and resilience. Resilience is often thought of as bouncing back or the ability to maintain or regain functioning after adversity. It's actually the most common response to trauma even when the stressor is severe. Post-traumatic growth, on the other hand, is about experiencing gains beyond pre-trauma functioning. It's about noticing meaningful psychological change because of the adversity or recovery process. Growth can show up as deepened relationships, new perspectives, or even a greater sense of purpose. >
