This study investigates whether large language models exhibit racial bias when providing psychiatric diagnoses and treatment recommendations for mental health patients. Researchers tested four AI models using ten patient cases under race-neutral, race-implied, and race-explicit conditions, with experts evaluating the outputs for bias. The main finding is that while diagnostic decisions remained largely unbiased, treatment plans were frequently inferior when patient race was mentioned, with one model showing particularly high levels of racial bias.
