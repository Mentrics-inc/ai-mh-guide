<!-- explainer from 2025-apa-dmh101-part2-clinician-guide-policy.pdf --> # What Are Digital Mental Health Tools? Digital mental health apps are smartphone or tablet programs designed to support emotional well-being. They range from self-guided meditation and mindfulness exercises to structured digital therapy modules, such as cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT). These tools can be used independently or alongside traditional care from a healthcare provider. # Why It Matters Demand for mental health services has surged, often outpacing the availability of human providers. Apps offer a way to increase access to care by reducing wait times and costs. They allow patients to track their moods in real-time and engage in therapeutic exercises anytime, anywhere. This accessibility is crucial for supporting treatment continuity and helping individuals manage symptoms between clinical visits. # Key Takeaways * **Support, Not Replacement:** Apps work best as supplements to professional care.
