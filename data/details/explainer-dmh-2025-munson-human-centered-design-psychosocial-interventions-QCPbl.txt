<!-- explainer from dmh-2025-munson-human-centered-design-psychosocial-interventions-transcript-yt-QCPblvfkmQg.txt · 15s --> # Making Mental Health Care Work in Real Life Mental health treatments often fail outside of research labs because they are too complex, don’t fit busy clinic workflows, or lose their effectiveness over time. This talk explores how **Human-Centered Design (HCD)** can fix these issues. By involving patients and clinicians in the design process, we can create interventions that are easier to use and more likely to succeed in real-world settings like primary care or schools. ### Why It Matters Standard treatments often suffer from "treatment drift," where clinicians unintentionally skip steps due to time pressures, reducing effectiveness. HCD helps identify these friction points early. It ensures that tools support, rather than hinder, the critical psychological mechanisms that make therapy work, ultimately improving patient outcomes and reducing costs. ### 3 Key Takeaways 1. **Use the "Discover, Design, Build, Test" Framework** Instead of building a solution immediately, first deeply understand the context and user needs.
