<!-- explainer from istss-2025-norman-moral-distress-workplace-transcript-yt-CwOgcTsuFDs.txt · 11s --> **Understanding Moral Distress in the Workplace** Moral distress occurs when your core values conflict with your work duties or organizational decisions. It arises when you feel forced to act against your ethics, fail to act when you should, or witness events that violate your moral standards. While moral distress is common, it can escalate into "moral injury," a more severe, long-lasting condition involving deep shame, guilt, and spiritual distress that impairs daily functioning. **Why It Matters** Unaddressed moral distress contributes to burnout, anxiety, sleep disturbances, and substance use. It can lead to emotional numbness and a loss of empathy, harming both individual well-being and team dynamics.
